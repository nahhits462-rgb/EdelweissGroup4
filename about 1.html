📍 About Nasugbu

Nasugbu is a first-class municipality in Batangas. Kilala ito sa magagandang beach, bundok, at isla. 2-3 hours lang mula Manila kaya favorite ito ng mga turista.

🌊 Why Visit Nasugbu?

✔ Calayo & Natipuan Beach - white sand
✔ Mt. Batulao & Mt. Talamitam - hiking
✔ Hamilo Coast - snorkeling & diving
✔ Fresh seafood & local food

Best time: November to May (dry season)
