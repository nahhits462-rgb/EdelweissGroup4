ABOUT NASUGBU FIESTA

Parangal kay St. Francis Xavier

December 2-3

What is Nasugbu Fiesta?

Ang Nasugbu Fiesta ay isang taunang pagdiriwang sa bayan ng Nasugbu, Batangas tuwing Disyembre 2-3. Ito ay isinasagawa bilang parangal kay St. Francis Xavier, ang patron saint ng bayan.

Ang pagdiriwang na ito ay nagpapakita ng pananampalataya, pagkakaisa, kultura, at tradisyon ng mga mamamayan. Ito rin ay panahon ng pasasalamat at pagsasama-sama ng pamilya, kaibigan, at buong komunidad.

Fiesta Information

  • Fiesta Date: December 2-3
  • Location: Nasugbu, Batangas
  • Patron Saint: St. Francis Xavier
  • Celebration: Religious and Cultural Fiesta

History of Nasugbu Fiesta

The history of Nasugbu is connected to the development of Christianity and the traditions of its local community.

1628 - Establishment

Jesuit missionaries helped introduce and spread Christianity in the community.

1852 - Parish Development

The religious community continued to develop under the devotion to St. Francis Xavier.

Present - Modern Celebration

Today, the fiesta remains an important celebration of faith, culture, unity, and community traditions.

Fiesta Activities

  • Religious Processions
  • Holy Mass and Prayers
  • Grand Parade
  • Cultural Dance Performances
  • Food Celebrations
  • Community Gatherings

Purpose of the Celebration

The Nasugbu Fiesta celebrates the faith and traditions of the people. It brings the community together and helps preserve the town's cultural heritage for future generations.
